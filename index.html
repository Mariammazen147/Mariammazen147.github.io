01 — About

Detail-oriented tester with a developer's background.

I started out building software, through the ALX Software Engineering program, internships, and a Computer Science degree at FCAI, Cairo University. Along the way I found that the part I enjoyed most was breaking things on purpose: finding the edge case nobody thought of and explaining exactly why it fails.

Today I work full time as a Software Tester at PoultrySync, where I design test cases from business requirements, run functional, regression and smoke testing before releases, and trace defects down to the data level so developers get precise root causes. Because I can read and write code, I work closely with developers and I'm comfortable with both manual and automated testing.
